THE ART BLOCK — copy this into the game

Paste into js/enemies.js (an enemy), js/dice.js (an engraving) or js/cardart.js (a card icon), replacing that entry's art: value. Paste an existing one in here and hit LOAD to edit it.

HOW IT WORKS

Tap to drop a point. Points join into a line as you go.
FINISH LINE ends that stroke and starts a fresh one — a shape is made of several separate strokes.
MIRROR draws the left side for you as you draw the right. Every enemy in the game is symmetrical, so leave this on for them.
EYE DOT places the glowing pips (eyes, cores, lights) — those are the e list, drawn solid rather than stroked.

The box is −1 to 1. Anything inside the dashed square is roughly the size of a normal enemy; the ring is where a big one reaches.
