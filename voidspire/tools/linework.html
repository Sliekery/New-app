SPRITE SHEET is what Unity, Godot, Phaser and Aseprite read — one strip plus a .json saying how to cut it. SVG stays sharp at any size and animates on its own. GIF plays anywhere and needs nothing installed; VIDEO is what Instagram takes. NOTHING as a ground gives you transparency, which GIF and PNG keep and video cannot.

THE ART BLOCK

One frame exports art: { p, e }. Two or more export art: { fps, frames: [...] }, which the renderer plays on a loop. Paste into js/enemies.js, js/dice.js or js/cardart.js. SAVE .JSON keeps layers and frames so you can carry on later — the art block alone cannot.
