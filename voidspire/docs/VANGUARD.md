# THE VANGUARD — class design

_Three archetypes, 66 cards, one die read three ways._

Written against measurement, not taste. The numbers this design answers to:

| Finding | Measured | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Drafting commons vs rares | 56.0% / 28.7% — inverted by 27 pts | rares ahead by 10 |
| Archetypes | 7, avg 6.7 cards each, 29% of pool unaligned | 3, ~22 cards each, nothing unaligned |
| Power share | 27% (Ironclad ~19%) | ~18% |
| Cards costing 2+ | 27% (Ironclad ~45%) | ~38% |
| Deck at run end | 26–29 cards | 20–24 (StS A20 wins average 20) |
| Deck seen per fight | 107% after the clock retune | keep |

Deck shape target: **60/40, two archetypes**. That is not a taste call — an Ironclad
starts at 10 cards and a winning deck ends near 20, so you add 13–15 cards across a
whole run. A 22-card archetype physically cannot fill a deck. Pure is unreachable;
the game is balanced around a main line plus a strong secondary.

---

## The spine: one die, three readings

Every archetype reads the same d20 differently. This is the class's identity and the
reason all three want a densely engraved die.

| | Reads the die by | Wants |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Marksmanship** | Aim STEERS the landing face | a dense run high on the die |
| **Bulwark** | its build cards scale with the FACE NUMBER you land on | high faces |
| **Stim** | misfires and low faces PAY you | low faces, and the jam |

Marksman and Bulwark both want to land high, so they ally naturally. Stim wants the
opposite, so a Marksman/Stim deck carries a real internal tension — which is exactly
what makes choosing a pair a decision instead of a formality.

---

## MARKSMANSHIP — you point the die

1. **Aim is ammunition.** 1 Aim = 1 face of travel. Hard cap **3 faces per roll**,
   however much you hold.
2. **The die steers itself.** After the roll it climbs to the **highest engraved face**
   it can reach within your Aim, and spends that much. Bare faces are never a
   destination, so steering is worth exactly as much as your engraving is good.
3. **A natural 1 cannot be steered.** A jam is a jam — face 1, misfire, always. This is
   what stops Marksmanship deleting variance, and it is the door Stim comes through.
4. **The band is read from the final face.** Aim no longer adds to `eff`. Climbing to 15
   earns SOLID as a *consequence* of steering. Crit means literally reaching face 20,
   which makes face 20 the most valuable square on the die.
5. **BURST N** — fires the landing face plus N−1 more round the ring at **100 / 60 / 35%**.
   The card states the direction.
6. **The cashouts RETAIN.** You hold them in hand until you are loaded. Aim has two
   sinks — steer now, or bank for the cashout — and that is the archetype's decision
   every single turn.

Steering is a Vanguard-only reading. Technomancer and Void Adept keep Aim as a band
bonus, which matters because the Adept's table is a U and he wants to go *down*.

---

## BULWARK — the wall is the weapon

1. **BULWARK is a wall, not Shield.** It does **not** expire at the start of your turn.
   It absorbs damage after Shield is gone. You build it across a fight.
2. **Its build cards scale with the face you land on.** Land on 17, bank a big wall.
   This is the die hook, and it is why Bulwark and Marksmanship fit together.
3. **You can detonate it.** Cards convert the wall into damage — some spend half and
   keep it standing, some consume the lot.
4. The tension is that **your wall is both your armour and your ammunition.** Firing it
   leaves you naked; holding it wastes it.

---

## STIM — the bottom of the die pays

1. **STIM is a stacking damage bonus you buy with HP.** It decays 1 per turn, so it is
   a burst resource, not a permanent stat.
2. **Missing HP is a scaling stat.** Cards read your wound: *"deal 5, +1 per 10% of Max
   HP missing"*.
3. **The die hook is the jam.** Misfires and low faces — the outcomes every other build
   throws away — pay Stim. The Vanguard's MISFIRE band stops being wasted and becomes
   the engine.
4. Stim is the only archetype that wants to roll badly, which is why it fights
   Marksmanship and why the bridge between them has to invert the steer.

---

## The bridge triangle

Three cards per pair, all uncommon or rare. They are the reason a two-archetype deck
beats a pure one, and finding one should read as the run telling you what to build.

**Marksman × Bulwark** — high rolls serve both
- **Firing Step** · U · 1 — Deal 4. BURST 2 ↑. Gain 4 Bulwark.
- **Loophole** · U · 1 — Power: each face your BURST hits adds 2 Bulwark.
- **Spotter's Wall** · R · 2 — Power: your wall adds its tenth to every BURST face.

**Bulwark × Stim** — build the wall out of yourself
- **Sandbag** · U · 1 — Lose 4 HP. Gain 12 Bulwark.
- **Scar Tissue** · U · 1 — Power: gain 2 Bulwark whenever you lose HP to your own cards.
- **Blood Cement** · R · 2 — Power: your wall is increased by 1% per 1% of Max HP missing.

**Marksman × Stim** — the tension pair, and it inverts the steer
- **Mainline** · U · 1 — Lose 3 HP. Gain 3 Aim.
- **Steady Hands** · U · 1 — Power: your die now DIVES to the lowest engraved face it
  can reach instead of climbing.
- **Dead Man's Trigger** · R · 2 — Power: below 40% Max HP, your attacks BURST 1 further.

## What has to change outside the card list

1. **Momentum is deleted.** BURST replaced it. Follow Through, Unload, Overrun, Rapid
   Fire, Trigger Discipline and Full Auto are all rewritten above.
2. **Deck size.** 22-card archetypes only pay off if decks land near 20–24, not 26–29.
   Card removal has to get cheaper and uncapped — which is also the credit sink the
   economy is missing, since 46% of run income is currently never spent. One change,
   two problems.
3. **Relic audit.** Most of the Vanguard's 21 class relics reference Momentum, Aim-as-
   band-bonus or Salvo/Exhaust. They need repointing at the three new resources.
4. **The gate.** `VS_PREFER=rare` must beat `VS_PREFER=common`, and a piloted draft must
   beat a random one by 10+. Neither is true today.
