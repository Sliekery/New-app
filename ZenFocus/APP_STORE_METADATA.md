## App Description

**Transform your focus. Simplify your day.**

ZenFocus is a beautifully minimal focus timer designed to help you work smarter, not harder. Built on the proven Pomodoro Technique, ZenFocus eliminates distractions and keeps you in the zone.

**WHY ZENFOCUS?**

Unlike bloated productivity apps, ZenFocus does one thing exceptionally well: it helps you focus. No complicated setup. No overwhelming features. Just start the timer and get to work.

**KEY FEATURES**

• **Beautiful Timer** - A clean, distraction-free interface that keeps you focused on what matters

• **Session Planning** - Stack focus and break sessions to create your perfect work schedule (unique feature!)

• **Smart Presets** - Choose from Classic Pomodoro, Deep Work, Quick Sprints, and more

• **Progress Tracking** - See your daily, weekly, and all-time focus statistics

• **Streak Counter** - Build consistency and watch your focus habit grow

• **Background Support** - Timer continues even when you switch apps

• **Customizable Durations** - Set your own focus and break lengths

• **Notifications** - Get alerted when sessions complete, even in the background

**SESSION PLANNING (EXCLUSIVE FEATURE)**

Plan your entire day's focus sessions in advance:
- "Morning Sprint" - 50 min work, 10 min break, repeat
- "Classic Pomodoro x4" - Traditional 25/5 pattern with long break
- "Writing Session" - Outline, draft, edit with built-in breaks
- Create your own custom session stacks

**PREMIUM FEATURES**

Upgrade once, own forever. No subscriptions.
- 8 beautiful accent color themes
- Detailed analytics and insights
- Pre-built session templates
- iCloud sync across devices
- Premium notification sounds

**PERFECT FOR**

✓ Students studying for exams
✓ Remote workers managing tasks
✓ Writers and creatives
✓ Anyone who wants to reduce distractions
✓ People who tried other timers and found them too complex

**PRIVACY FOCUSED**

ZenFocus respects your privacy:
- No account required
- No ads (ever)
- No tracking
- All data stays on your device

Start focusing today. Download ZenFocus now.
